Coordinator independently reviewed and corrected its suggested spurious interrupt EOI sequence: phantom master IRQ7 receives no EOI; phantom slave IRQ15 gets master-only EOI. See ADR-m9-3-pic-foundation.md for PIC/APIC comparison and vector ownership.

## Code and ownership
`src/kernel/x86-pic.h/.c` introduces a single-CPU, caller-owned state with testable IO callbacks and a privileged hardware adapter. Initialization masks both chips, remaps master/slave to vectors 32–47, preserves all bits masked, and tracks shadow masks. IRQ2 is cascade-only. Invalid arguments issue no port writes. Mask/unmask of slave IRQs updates master cascade consistently. EOI handling checks ISR for spurious IRQ7/15; all operations are bounded and do not allocate.

`src/kernel/main.c` initializes the fully masked PIC after IDT installation, before memory services. F12 PIT channel 2 and PS/2/serial polling are unchanged. No IRQ handler vectors or `sti` are installed.
