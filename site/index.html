A computer you can understand

SpiffOS isn't a Commodore emulator or a retro-themed Linux distribution. It's an independent x86-64 operating system designed to boot directly into a programming environment. Type a few lines of BASIC, make something happen, and keep exploring.

PRINT "HELLO, SPIFFOS!"

Simple enough to understand. Powerful enough to create.

Designed for creators

Modern BASIC

Structured, line-number-free programming, an interpreter and a built-in native compiler.

Graphics and sprites

Full-color pixels, tile maps, animation, layers and built-in sprite collisions.

Music and sound

Thirty-two virtual voices per workspace for instruments, effects and background music.

Devices and connections

USB serial, full-duplex audio input/output, wired TCP/IP and hardware-independent interfaces.

Protected workspaces

Independent BASIC programs and a workspace model designed to stay responsive.

Learning by doing

Documented virtual registers, PEEK/POKE, practical examples and accessible manuals.
